What you get

Everything your tournament needs, live

Scoring, streaming, clips, highlights and reports — one platform, all from a phone or a laptop.

✂️ Auto clips

Every 4, 6 & wicket — clipped in HD, automatically

Each boundary and wicket is cut straight from the live stream and filed under the players who made it. Nobody scrubs through hours of video.

  • Find any clip by player name — on the scorecard and the tournament page
  • Filter a player's sixes, fours or wickets, match by match
  • Download one clip, or all of them as a single MP4
⚡ Instant highlights

A player's highlights — edited and ready in one tap

Pick a player and get their fours, sixes and wickets joined into one edited video: a title card with their team and figures, a tag on every shot and an animated wagon wheel.

  • 16:9 for YouTube, or 9:16 for Reels, Shorts and WhatsApp Status
  • Runs, balls, sixes, fours and strike rate on the title card
  • The wagon wheel shows exactly where every shot went
📄 Auto match report

A stunning, professional match report for every match

  • Result, player of the match and best performers on page one
  • Wagon wheels for both teams and for the top scorer
  • Full scorecards, fall of wickets, partnerships and squads
Download a sample report PDF · 1.1 MB
🏏 Live scoreboard

A live scorecard fans actually follow

Every ball reaches the public scorecard the moment it's scored — on any phone, with no app to install.

  • Ball-by-ball commentary with a wagon wheel for every over
  • Batting and bowling cards, partnerships and the required rate
  • A live "watching now" count beside every match
🏆 Tournament manager

Run the whole tournament from one page

Fixtures, results, the points table and the leaderboards update themselves after every match.

  • Upcoming fixtures, and every result with its full scorecard
  • Points table with net run rate and each team's form
  • Orange & Purple Cap leaderboards, player and team history
📺 Broadcast graphics

TV-style graphics on your YouTube stream

🖥️ LED big screen

Your score on the big screen at the ground

A full-screen LED scoreboard runs from the same live feed — big, high-contrast numbers that read from the boundary.

  • Score, overs, run rates and the chase equation
  • Batters, bowler and this over, ball by ball
  • Any LED wall or TV with a browser — nothing to install
